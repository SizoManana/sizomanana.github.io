03 / Get the actual number

Build, verify and download

The entire corpus is fetched from the public KJV text, then encoded in your browser with the same 32-bit integer arithmetic model as the original project. You can download the complete number as a reversible .bib1 archive.

Connecting to source...

Source corpus: aruljohn/Bible-kjv (66-book Protestant King James Version; no Apocrypha). Exact representation includes book/verse labels, punctuation, UTF-8 encoding, newline layout, the static frequency model and an EOF symbol. This is lossless compression—not a way of storing millions of bits in a short decimal.
