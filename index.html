What I bring

Product and UX

Information architecture, user flows, wireframing, interface hierarchy, component systems, interaction states, responsive behaviour, accessibility considerations and developer handoff.

Research and optimisation

End-to-end CRO programme strategy and delivery, including heuristic evaluation, behavioural analytics, session recordings, market and vertical research, segmentation, hypotheses, prioritisation, GA4/GTM instrumentation, experimentation and post-launch validation.

The distinction I care aboutBefore-and-after movement is evidence of change, not automatic proof of causation. Where there is no controlled experiment, I treat the result as directional and document the limitations.

About the person behind the work

A commercially minded design practice

I moved from finance administration into digital design, lead-generation work, UX/UI, CRO and enterprise product design. That path is why I naturally connect interface decisions to business outcomes.

I am most useful in teams where the role crosses boundaries: messy problem framing, stakeholder context, complex interfaces, optimisation work and the evidence needed to defend a decision.
