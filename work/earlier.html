Earlier work

Where the conversion instinct started

Landing pages, homepages and campaign creative from SearchKings Africa between 2022 and 2025. I keep this work separate from the recent case studies because the measurement discipline was not yet there. What it shows is the earlier pattern: diagnose the hesitation, make the offer clearer, remove dead ends and give the visitor an obvious next action.

ContextMostly paid-traffic and lead-generation work where every weak landing page made media spend less efficient.
StrengthOffer hierarchy, objection handling, CTA focus and responsive craft.
LimitI did not own robust analytics, baselines or controlled testing on these projects.
Why show itIt makes the progression into research-led CRO and product thinking visible rather than pretending it appeared overnight.

Early diagnosis

Nutaria, a homepage transformation

The homepage did not immediately show the product or its sourcing, lacked a clear route to enquiry or purchase, could not communicate changing stock well and was weak on mobile. At the time I wrote those problems down before designing, which is the earliest version of the audit-first habit I use now.

Why this one stays

The diagnosis was thin compared with my current work and had no behavioural baseline behind it, but the sequence was correct: define what the page is failing to do before deciding what it should look like.

Objection handling

Nakavango Conservation Programme

A volunteer recruitment page near Victoria Falls for visitors making a high-consideration decision involving several weeks of time, travel and cost. The application form sits in the hero for qualified ad traffic, trust appears immediately beneath it, and the FAQ answers the questions most likely to block commitment: eligibility, dates, free time, vaccinations and visas.

The objection is often the content. Once the real hesitation is known, the page structure becomes easier to justify.

Decision support

MORE Field Guide College

The audience is choosing between an adventure and a career change. Accreditation and human contact therefore sit beside the enquiry route, because credibility and the ability to ask a real person a question are part of the conversion experience for a high-consideration course.

Wayfinding under anxiety

Paediatric Liver and Transplant Services South Africa

This service had no website. Parents needed to understand what conditions were treated, who would treat their child and what to do next, often while stressed and on a phone. The hero therefore behaves more like wayfinding than marketing: get a referral, prepare, book the next appointment.

What I would do differently

I would test that three-step pattern with actual parents. This is the project on the page where an incorrect assumption would matter most, and reasoning alone is not enough evidence.

Progression

What this period actually taught me

  • Paid traffic makes unclear pages expensive. A weak experience converts media budget into abandonment.
  • The objection belongs in the interface. Visas, accreditation, referral routes, stock and eligibility are not side notes when they determine whether somebody continues.
  • Reasoning beats taste. Writing the rationale down makes design easier to challenge and hand over.
  • The missing piece was measurement. These projects do not have the baselines and validation I would require today, which is why the recent work sits above them in the portfolio hierarchy.

Why this page is deliberately secondary: it is career progression evidence, not outcome evidence. The newer case studies are where research, instrumentation and validation become part of the work rather than a retrospective wish.
