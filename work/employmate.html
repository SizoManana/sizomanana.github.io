Conversion rate optimisation

Two audiences, one site, and neither of them well served

Employmate is a global recruitment partner specialising in ethical international hiring. The traffic was healthy and the rankings were good. The conversion was not, and the reason was structural rather than cosmetic.

My roleCRO and UX, as part of the delivery team
The problemEmployers hiring and candidates seeking work were sent down the same path
PublishedCase study on evara.co, which credits me by name

A site cannot serve two opposite intents with one journey

An employer wants to know whether you can find them people, legally, in countries they do not understand. A candidate wants to know whether you will find them work and treat them properly. Those are not variations on a theme, they are different products with different anxieties, and a single funnel serving both ends up being vague enough to convert neither.

The fix was a persona switch, giving each audience its own path from the first screen, then rebuilding the landing pages, CTAs and forms around the intent that switch declares.

What the work covered

  • The persona switch and the content architecture behind it
  • Journey friction removal across both paths
  • Landing pages, CTAs and forms rebuilt around declared intent rather than assumed intent
  • Tracking and analytics implemented so improvement could continue after handover
  • HubSpot CMS training so the internal team could run it themselves

The part I am most pleased with is the training

We handed over CMS training as part of the engagement. It is the least glamorous line in the case study and probably the most valuable, because a conversion improvement that decays the moment the agency leaves was never really an improvement. It was a rental.

On what I can and cannot tell you

The published case study describes persona specific experience, a streamlined journey and optimised conversion touchpoints. It does not publish conversion figures, and I am not going to invent any here.

If you want to interrogate the reasoning, the mechanism or what I would measure to prove it worked, ask me and I will walk you through it properly. That is a better conversation than a percentage you cannot check.
